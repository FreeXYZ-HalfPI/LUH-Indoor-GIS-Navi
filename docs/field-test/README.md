# Field test

The indoor routes are traced from floor plans and nobody has walked them yet.
A field test walks a sample of routes in each mapped building, records what
is wrong, and ends with floors marked as **verified on site** in the app.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| this README | the protocol: how to run a test round |
| [1101.md](1101.md), [3101.md](3101.md), [3109.md](3109.md), [3110.md](3110.md), [3408.md](3408.md) | checklists: 40 routes per building, one app link per route |
| [routes.csv](routes.csv) | the same routes as a table, to collect the results in a spreadsheet |

The checklists and the table are generated by `python3 tools/field_test.py`
(options: `--per-building`, `--buildings`, `--seed`). The same seed always
gives the same routes; use a new seed for a second round.

## The routes

Every building gets a mix of four kinds:

| Kind | Tests | Share |
|---|---|---|
| **entrance** | from outside to a room on every mapped floor: the entrance, then stairs or lifts | one per floor |
| **same-floor** | corridors and turns; the two rooms are at least 20 m apart | ~40% of the rest |
| **cross-floor** | stairs; every 4th route has *avoid steps* on, which tests the lifts | ~35% |
| **cross-building** | the way out, the walk outside and the way in, to one of the two nearest mapped buildings | ~25% |

Half of the endpoints are public rooms (lecture and seminar rooms), the rooms
students look for most.

## Before the test

- **Team:** pairs work best. One person follows the phone as a stranger would,
  the other reads the checklist and takes notes. 5–10 students cover all five
  buildings in a day or two.
- **Time:** count 4–6 minutes per route inside a building and 10–15 minutes
  per cross-building route: about 3–4 hours for one building's 40 routes.
  Split a checklist between pairs.
- **Phone:** charged, with the app opened once on Wi-Fi, so it works without
  mobile data inside the building. Note the phone and browser on the checklist.
- **Checklist:** print the building's page, or open it on GitHub on a second
  phone (the route numbers are links that open the route in the app).
- **Permission:** tell the building's caretaker (Hausmeister) or institute
  office about the test. Rooms do not have to be entered: arriving at the
  right door counts.

## During the test, for each route

1. Open the route with its link. Check that *from*, *to* and *avoid steps*
   match the checklist.
2. Go to the start: the start room's door, or, for *outside*, the building
   entrance the directions name.
3. Follow the directions step by step **as a stranger**. Do not use what you
   know about the building; if a step is unclear, that is a finding too.
4. Tick a box only when that part was right:

   | Check | Right means |
   |---|---|
   | **entrance / exit** | the named entrance exists, is open and is on the named side; for routes inside one floor, tick it |
   | **turns & distances** | every *left / right / straight on* is right and the step counts are roughly right (±30%) |
   | **stairs / lift** | the stairs or lift are where the directions say and reach the named floor; for same-floor routes, tick it |
   | **arrived** | you stand at the door of the destination room |

5. If anything is wrong, write the **step number** and what is wrong in
   *notes*, e.g. "step 4: the corridor ends at a locked fire door". Then, if
   you can, tap **Report a problem with this route** under the directions and
   paste the note: the issue gets the route's codes by itself, never names.
   Write the issue number into the checklist.

Do not photograph people, office doors with name plates, or notices.

## After the test (maintainers)

1. Copy the results into `routes.csv` (one row per route; `entrance_ok`,
   `turns_ok`, `vertical_ok`, `arrived`: `yes` or `no`; `issue`: the GitHub
   issue number).
2. Fix the data in the building's `data/indoor/<building>/source.json`:
   missing or wrong corridors, stairs, lifts, entrances. Rebuild with
   `python3 tools/build_indoor.py build <building>` and re-walk the routes
   that failed.
3. **Mark a floor as verified** when every route that touched the floor
   passed (after the fixes) and at least 3 routes touched it. Add the floor
   with the date of the check to `"verified"` in `source.json`, then:

   ```bash
   python3 tools/build_indoor.py status <building>
   python3 tools/coverage.py
   ```

   Details: [Marking a floor as verified](../INDOOR_FORMAT.md#marking-a-floor-as-verified).
   The app then tags those floors *✓ checked on site* in the directions.
4. Remove a floor from `"verified"` again when it is rebuilt from new plans or
   the building changes.
